## Optional and development dependencies

The ordinary browser workshop runs with the bundled Three.js runtime and the
Python standard-library server. Playwright and its browser binaries are
development tools installed separately for browser tests. Playwright 1.62.1
and playwright-core use Apache-2.0; the optional fsevents dependency uses MIT.
Their upstream notices apply to those distributions and downloaded browsers.

The optional IPC reference backend installs the packages pinned in
[ipc-probe.requirements.txt](scripts/ipc-probe.requirements.txt). Those packages
and their compiled dependencies retain their upstream licenses; the project's
MIT license does not relicense them. See the installed distributions for their
complete notices.

Newton/Warp comparison environments and local virtual environments are not
bundled in this repository. Research links in the documentation are references,
not redistributed commercial tools, models, or assets.
